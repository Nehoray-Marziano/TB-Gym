# Talia design context

## Overview

Talia is a Hebrew, right-to-left studio booking app, primarily used on phones. The public introduction carries the brand; the member home helps a returning member see her next workout and find the schedule. Product rules and the route inventory are maintained in `../TALIA-APP-HANDOFF.md`.

The home direction (October 1, 2026) keeps one prominent workout card, a balance row, and persistent navigation. The first sparse implementation was rejected as empty and lacking personality. Preserve the intro's expressive Hebrew typography, sage and terracotta palette, sun and botanical composition. Simpler navigation must not mean a generic or visually vacant page. Avoid repeated destination tiles and promotional content on the member home.

## Colors

Runtime ownership remains in `src/app/globals.css`: the single canonical green-brownish boutique palette defines the `--studio-*` values (`#e9eadc` canvas, `#162218` deep ink, `#8b8e6f` brand, `#cbd3aa` accent text, `#c37a61` terracotta coral). Theme switching has been removed to guarantee rock-solid visual stability across all devices. The home uses `--studio-canvas` and `--studio-ink` for the page, `--studio-deep` and `--studio-deep-contrast` for its focal card, `--studio-accent-text` for the card label and empty-state action, and `--studio-muted` for secondary text. No extraneous color overrides are permitted.

## Typography

`src/app/layout.tsx` loads Varela Round, consumed through the global font variables. Use one greeting, one section label, and the workout title; show the appointment date and time only once. Hebrew flows RTL; times are isolated LTR and user-provided workout titles use automatic direction. Long names and titles wrap rather than disappear.

## Layout

The introduction and member home each own a `100dvh` viewport. `src/components/home/home-layout.css` implements their sizing: the introduction reserves the header and actions around its flexible hero; member home gives the extra height to the greeting above a content-sized workout card and balance row. The workout card reserves up to 300px (32% of viewport height on smaller phones), grows only when its content needs it, and must not stretch into every available pixel. The introduction is at most 544px wide; member home is at most 608px wide, with adaptive 20–32px gutters. Their main elements are size-query containers, so typography, emblems, controls, and spacing respond to usable height and content width rather than phone width alone. Keep body text at least 14px and primary touch targets at least 44px. Long names and titles must wrap without widening the grid or being clipped.

Safe-area padding is part of the available-height calculation. Member home reserves 96px plus the bottom safe area for the shared 72px navigation dock and its clearance. Wide screens with heights of 540px or less use two columns so landscape can fit without reducing every element. Other routes retain their existing scroll ownership. Verify text bounds, section overlap, navigation clearance, and actual document scrolling with `node scripts/check-home-layout.mjs` against a local development server.

The fixed bottom navigation owns Home, Schedule, and Account. It stays available on entry and near the top, slides away after 32px of downward travel, and returns after 12px of upward travel. Direction thresholds ignore jitter, horizontal strips, dialog scrolling, and elastic overscroll. Route changes, resizing, and keyboard navigation reveal it. Text entry and active modals suppress it; it returns when they close. Scroll-hidden links remain keyboard and screen-reader accessible; Tab reveals the dock before focus arrives. Suppressed navigation is inert. Reduced motion disables its transition. Route scrollers reserve stable bottom padding and scroll-padding even while the dock is hidden. The card owns one contextual action: My workouts when booked, Find a workout when empty. Membership is a compact secondary link beside the balance. Installation stays in Account. Administrators retain a small header link.

The public introduction reserves the studio emblem's intrinsic dimensions before its image loads. Its fixed shell follows the visual viewport before paint, on resize, and on app resume, falling back to the window height. Safe-area padding remains inside that height so both sign-in buttons and the reassurance footer stay visible after a logged-out PWA cold launch. Pinch zoom preserves the layout size. `scripts/check-pwa-startup.mjs` checks the logged-out start URL, delayed logo loading, relaunch, safe areas, and a visible viewport shorter than the CSS viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

The greeting has a decorative terracotta sun and existing leaf artwork beside large personal typography. The workout card carries tonal depth and a soft shadow; a separate date column gives booked sessions a recognizable appointment composition. The balance remains an unboxed row, with a larger numeral and a fine baseline. Space should frame these elements, not dominate the screen.

## Shapes

The workout card uses three 30px corners and one 10px corner, echoing the studio's existing asymmetric surfaces. Rounded controls and the circular appointment arrow soften the composition. Botanical art stays decorative and inaccessible to assistive technology.

## Components

- Subscription is mobile only: an animated description and see-plans action reveal native horizontal cards centered on 8, with 4 physically right and 12 left. No bottom arrows or tier pagination. The centered card dominates; smaller edge previews remain visible. Cached scroll geometry drives inner-card scale/opacity and studio-token scene weights in one animation frame, immediately following the finger. Snap wrappers and touch targets stay stable; payment ownership and accessible selection settle after touch/momentum end. Native scrolling owns reveal and swipes, with timer fallbacks, cancellation recovery, and stable viewport units. Avoid nested springs, per-frame React selection/layout reads, animated SVG paths, and ambient loops. PaymentModal owns the tier-accent receipt sheet; its scrollable receipt and persistent footer provide copy instructions, manual approval context, and a right-to-left Bit confirmation slider. Only full slide plus release opens Bit; preserve tap/keyboard alternatives, cancelled-slide return, blocked-popup retry, duplicate prevention, modal focus, and reduced motion. Colors and font remain canonical studio tokens. Page geometry and verification live in design-system/talia-studio/pages/subscription.md. Bit opening does not verify payment; workouts follow Talia approval.

- Booking Schedule (לוח אימונים) is mobile only: V2 features a sticky frosted-glass header with live ticket balance and manual refresh; a native 14-day horizontal calendar strip with status indicators and quick-day filtering; segmented filter chips for all/available/my-bookings; asymmetric luxury boutique session cards with real-time capacity dot meters and urgency badges; and native bottom sheets for booking confirmation and cancellation with automatic ticket balance refund guarantee. Haptics and celebratory confetti reinforce successful reservations. Page documentation lives in `design-system/talia-studio/pages/book.md`.

- `src/components/home/TraineeDashboard.tsx` owns greeting, upcoming-workout rendering, and balance summary; `GymStoreProvider` owns balance and profile data.
- In-app branding reuses `StudioLogo` and the clean `initials_logo.svg` TB mark without the tagline. The public introduction retains the full studio signature.
- Intro Google and email entry buttons reuse `LiquidGlassButton` and the dock's mist material: moderate fixed blur, restrained refraction, bright rims, and static diagonal reflections over translucent fills. Google uses a whiter pearl tint; email reveals more of the sage background. `studio-welcome-auth-button` variants in `home-layout.css` own the material, 22px corners, dark labels, and visible keyboard focus. Keep the provider mark, existing Google/OTP handlers, responsive 48–64px targets, and reduced-motion feedback.
- `src/components/BottomNav.tsx` remains the shared navigation owner. Labels are בית, לוח אימונים, חשבון. My bookings remains in the Home navigation family.
- `src/lib/navigation.ts` owns the ordered member/admin destinations and active-route families. `PageEntrance` follows their physical RTL order: pages enter from the left when selecting a tab to the left and from the right when selecting one to the right, including browser Back/Forward. The shared CSS entrance uses 16–24px of responsive horizontal travel over 360ms with a gentle settle, paired with a separate 160ms fade from 45% opacity to full contrast. There is no scale or vertical movement; initial loads and navigation within one family use only the short fade. Animation fill releases the page transform after completion. Reduced motion removes both animations. The dock stays outside the animated page and route scroll ownership remains unchanged.
- Member navigation is a detached, fully rounded glass dock, at most 23rem wide with 16px side clearance and 12px plus device safe-area clearance below. Reuse `LiquidGlass` for the surface; `studio-navigation-*` in `globals.css` owns its business-specific elevation and active colors. The body is visibly see-through mist glass: a sparse cream tint and moderate fixed blur let underlying card shapes, colors, and movement remain recognizable. Reflective rims, restrained edge refraction, and static diagonal light catches define the glass rather than a milky fill. Glyphs use local contrast halos over dark content; avoid making the whole dock opaque to protect labels. Avoid ambient shimmer or animated blur. Each labeled tab has a 60px minimum height. The translucent forest selected capsule has a polished upper edge and cream text, and moves between tabs with a short spring; reduced-motion users get an immediate change. Opening a native action dialog immediately starts the dock's 200ms downward retreat; it remains isolated below the viewport until the dialog finishes closing. Keep existing page bottom padding, which reserves room for the 72px dock and its lower clearance.
- Existing Next Links own navigation; links have focus, hover, and pressed feedback. `useMemberNavigation` owns dock visibility; `[data-member-scroll]` explicitly identifies each member route scroll owner. `StudioModal` owns booking and cancellation overlays on Schedule and My workouts: native top-layer dialogs provide focus trapping and inert background, restore trigger focus, initially focus the safe return action, guard dismissal while busy, and lock route scrolling. Content is bounded to the visual viewport and safe areas with a scrollable body and persistent action footer. `PaymentModal` remains the existing payment-dialog owner. Cancellation cutoff, ticket refunds, and booking mutations retain their existing owners and rules.
- Loading reserves the workout content region and announces progress. A failed request is distinct from an empty booking list. Empty copy makes no assertion about available class capacity.
- Existing toast, theme, and account-installation owners remain unchanged.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Keep one workout-related action inside the home card.
- Keep balance legible without turning it into another competing card.
- Do not add quick-action tiles that repeat the card or bottom navigation.
- Keep the member home strictly zero-scroll on standard mobile viewports; omit bottom tips/notes that cause scrolling.
